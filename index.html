      Distance and the starting direction are measured on the WGS84 ellipsoid, the Earth's shape as GPS uses it (Vincenty's formula, checked against GeographicLib to the metre), from town centre to town centre. Roads and flight paths are longer. The flight time is fitted to the median scheduled time of 5,955 US routes (BTS, August 2025): 45 minutes for taxi, climb and descent, 820 km/h, faster past 4,000 km, and the jet stream's pull between 25° and 60° of latitude. Half of those routes land within 5 minutes of it. Abroad it is checked only against a handful of long-haul timetables, so treat it as a guide there. Time zones come from your browser's own rules. Jet lag days use the average adjustment rates in the CDC Yellow Book (1 h a day after flying east, 1.5 h west), taking the shorter way round the clock; people vary, and this is not medical advice.
